# Privacy Policy — HowRead Pro

HowRead Pro is an ebook reader application. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page informs visitors of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information. Except as described in this policy, we will not use or share your information with anyone.

This policy consists of two parts: the **General Privacy Statement** (applies to all channel versions) and the **Huawei Platform Privacy Statement** (applies to the Huawei AppGallery channel version).

## General Privacy Statement (applies to all versions)

HowRead Pro is first and foremost an offline reader:

* The documents you open (PDF, EPUB, MOBI, DjVu, FB2, TXT, CBZ, etc.) are stored and processed **locally on your device**. They are never uploaded to us.
* Reading progress, bookmarks, annotations and settings are stored locally.
* The app requests storage permission only to scan and read the document folders you configure.
* We operate no servers that collect user data, and the app contains no analytics or crash-reporting components that report to the developer.

**Network services are configured by you and connect directly to your own servers**

* **Sync (WebDAV)**: when enabled, reading progress and bookmarks are transferred **directly between your devices and your own WebDAV server**. We do not operate, access, or store any sync data; server credentials are stored on your device only.
* **Online reading (WebDAV / SMB / SFTP)**: when opening remote books, the app connects directly to the server you configured. Downloaded caches are stored locally and can be cleared at any time in the app.
* **AI features (Pro)**: only after you configure a large-model service yourself will selected text or questions be sent to **the service address you specified**; the API key is encrypted on your device and excluded from backup and sync.

**Channel differences**

* **F-Droid version**: contains no advertising SDK, no in-app purchases and no vendor service components.
* **Huawei AppGallery version**: contains Huawei In-App Purchases and Huawei Ads components — see the [Huawei Platform Privacy Statement](#huawei-platform-privacy-statement-huawei-appgallery--appgallery-channel-version) below.
* If other channel versions include advertising or third-party components, they will be described in this policy.

**Log Data**

In the case of an error in the app, data and information may be collected on your phone called Log Data. This Log Data may include information such as your device IP address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing our Service, the time and date of your use, and other statistics. The Service does not collect or upload this data through any developer-operated channel.

**Cookies**

This Service does not use "cookies" explicitly. However, the app may use third-party code and libraries that use "cookies" to collect information and improve their services. You have the option to either accept or refuse these cookies and know when a cookie is being sent to your device.

**Service Providers**

We may employ third-party companies and individuals due to the following reasons:

* To facilitate our Service;
* To provide the Service on our behalf;
* To perform Service-related services; or
* To assist us in analyzing how our Service is used.

These third parties have access to your Personal Information only to perform the tasks assigned to them on our behalf, and they are obligated not to disclose or use the information for any other purpose.

**Security**

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

**Links to Other Sites**

This Service may contain links to other sites. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. Note that these external sites are not operated by us. Therefore, we strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of these websites. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

**Children's Privacy**

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete the relevant data. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we will be able to take necessary actions.

## Huawei Platform Privacy Statement (Huawei AppGallery channel version)

This section applies to the HowRead Pro version distributed through Huawei AppGallery. In addition to the general statement above, this version contains the following Huawei components:

### Huawei In-App Purchases (HMS IAP)

* Purpose: purchasing and restoring the Pro feature unlock (a non-consumable in-app product).
* Ordering and payment are completed by HUAWEI ID and Huawei's payment service; your payment instrument details (such as bank cards) never pass through the app and are never accessible to us.
* The app receives only the purchase result (product ID, order ID, purchase time and status), used to unlock features and to restore your purchase after reinstalling or switching devices. This information is **stored locally on your device only** — we operate no servers, so it is never uploaded.
* Personal data processing during payment is governed by [Huawei's Privacy Statement](https://consumer.huawei.com/en/privacy/privacy-statement/).

### Huawei Ads (HMS Ads Kit)

* Purpose: displaying banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads in the app, served by Huawei's ad service.
* For ad delivery and measurement, the Huawei Ads SDK may process device identifiers (such as OAID / AAID), device information (model, OS version), network information (IP address and approximate location) and ad interaction data in accordance with its own policies. Such data is processed independently by Huawei and is governed by [Huawei's Privacy Statement](https://consumer.huawei.com/en/privacy/privacy-statement/).
* You can manage ad personalization in your system settings (such as "Limit personalized ads" or "Reset advertising identifier (OAID)"); the app does not currently provide a separate in-app ad personalization toggle.

### Huawei services NOT integrated

* The app does not integrate HMS Analytics Kit, Huawei Push Kit or AGConnect cloud services, and does not collect or report any data through them.
