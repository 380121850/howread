# Huawei Platform Privacy Statement — HowReadPro

This statement applies to the HowReadPro version distributed through **Huawei AppGallery**. It supplements the [General Privacy Policy]({{ site.baseurl }}/PrivacyPolicy/general), and both apply together.

Last updated: 2026-09-20

In addition to the general policy, this channel version contains the following Huawei components:

## Huawei In-App Purchases (HMS IAP)

* Purpose: purchasing and restoring the Pro feature unlock (a non-consumable in-app product).
* Ordering and payment are completed by HUAWEI ID and Huawei's payment service; your payment instrument details (such as bank cards) never pass through the app and are never accessible to us.
* The app receives only the purchase result (product ID, order ID, purchase time and status), used to unlock features and to restore your purchase after reinstalling or switching devices. This information is **stored locally on your device only** — we operate no servers, so it is never uploaded.
* Personal data processing during payment is governed by [Huawei's Privacy Statement](https://consumer.huawei.com/en/privacy/privacy-statement/).

## Huawei Ads (HMS Ads Kit)

* Purpose: displaying banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads in the app, served by Huawei's ad service.
* For ad delivery and measurement, the Huawei Ads SDK may process device identifiers (such as OAID / AAID), device information (model, OS version), network information (IP address and approximate location) and ad interaction data in accordance with its own policies. Such data is processed independently by Huawei and is governed by [Huawei's Privacy Statement](https://consumer.huawei.com/en/privacy/privacy-statement/).
* You can manage ad personalization in your system settings (such as "Limit personalized ads" or "Reset advertising identifier (OAID)"); the app does not currently provide a separate in-app ad personalization toggle.

## Huawei services NOT integrated

* The app does not integrate HMS Analytics Kit, Huawei Push Kit or AGConnect cloud services, and does not collect or report any data through them.

## Changes to This Statement

We may update this statement from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
